Vision & Mission

Vision: To become a media and publishing platform that develops various digital projects (such as media, magazines, and digital systems) to present information, creativity, and innovation within a single ecosystem.
Mission: Develop various digital projects (including Kafkapages, Kazine, Kaboom, and others), provide a media and publication platform for everyone, build technology features such as digital banking (Chobank) and directory systems (Chonnect) that make life easier for all people, integrate multiple services into one seamless digital ecosystem.
