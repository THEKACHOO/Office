The Kachoo

The Kafka family legacy evolves through Kachoo — a visionary ecosystem where media, publishing, and digital innovation converge. Rooted in storytelling heritage, Kachoo expands into digital projects, creative magazines, and integrated technology.

Kachoo represents the future of digital media: from editorial platforms (Kafkapages, Kazine, Kaboom) to breakthrough systems like Chobank (digital banking) and Chonnect (directory ecosystem). All unified under a single black & white aesthetic, with seamless motion and clarity.

Vision & Mission

Vision: To become a media and publishing platform that develops various digital projects (such as media, magazines, and digital systems) to present information, creativity, and innovation within a single ecosystem.
  • Mission: Develop various digital projects (including Kafkapages, Kazine, Kaboom, and others)
  • Provide a media and publication platform for everyone
  • Build technology features such as digital banking (Chobank) and directory systems (Chonnect) that make life easier for all people
  • Integrate multiple services into one seamless digital ecosystem
