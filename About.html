The Kachoo

Kachoo is a digital media and news company that develops projects including magazines, news platforms, and integrated digital systems. Launched on January 2026 in South Jakarta, Indonesia.

Vision & Mission

Vision: To become a media and publishing platform that develops various projects in media, magazines, and digital systems to present information, creativity, and innovation within a single ecosystem.
Mission: Develop various digital projects like Kafkapages, Kazine, Kaboom, and more, provide a media and publication platform for everyone, build technology features such as digital banking in Chobank and directory systems in Chonnect that make easier system for everyone.
